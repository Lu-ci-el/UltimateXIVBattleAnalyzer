# Support development

[Home](../README.en.md) · [日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md)

I am building UXBA to make replays easier to organize, revisit, and anonymize before sharing.

Keeping it working costs money and time.  
Game updates require adjustments, and recordings from different generations need careful testing.  
I can fund updates myself through December 2026.  
Updates from January 2027 onward, 8.0 support, and further work on Full Remake will depend on community support.

If UXBA is useful to you, a contribution at a level you are comfortable with would help keep the project going.  
Messages of encouragement are welcome too.

- [Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lost2)
- [Support on OFUSE](https://ofuse.me/lost)

## Bugs and unsupported DATs

Investigation, fixes, and support for additional DAT formats depend on funding and available development time.  
Depending on those resources, a response may take time, or a problem may remain unresolved.

Donations are not required to report a problem, contribute evidence, or ask a question.  
Reports and donations do not guarantee a fix, a new feature, priority, or a deadline.

If conversion, battle extraction, or party-list organization do not work correctly, send the original DAT through the [Dropbox file submission form](https://www.dropbox.com/request/gkofh48datowq9uozjif). Include a text file with the UXBA version, the operation you tried, and the symptoms or error message. If you have opened an issue, include its URL too.

Please use Issues for bugs and Discussions for questions and ideas.  
See the [reporting and file sharing guide](https://github.com/Lu-ci-el/UltimateXIVBattleAnalyzer/blob/main/.github/SUPPORT.md#english) for details.
