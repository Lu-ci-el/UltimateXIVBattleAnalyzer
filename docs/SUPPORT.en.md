# Development support

[Home](../README.en.md) · [日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md)

UltimateXIVBattleAnalyzer is generally free. Development, adapting to game updates, and analyzing additional DATs take time, and continuing is difficult without support.

Future work and coverage, including Full Remake, depend on overall demand and support. Decisions are not based on an individual donation amount.

- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lost2)
- [OFUSE](https://ofuse.me/lost)

Support is not required to report problems or ask questions. Donations do not guarantee a fix, priority, successful restoration, or a deadline.

Use [Issues](https://github.com/Lu-ci-el/UltimateXIVBattleAnalyzer/issues/new/choose) for bug reports, [Discussions](https://github.com/Lu-ci-el/UltimateXIVBattleAnalyzer/discussions) for usage questions, and the [submission form](https://www.dropbox.com/request/gkofh48datowq9uozjif) when a DAT is needed.
