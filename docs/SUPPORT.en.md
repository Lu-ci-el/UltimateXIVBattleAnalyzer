# Support development

[Home](../README.en.md) · [日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md)

I am building UXBA to make replays easier to organize, revisit, and anonymize before sharing.

Keeping it working costs money and time. Game updates require adjustments, and recordings from different generations need careful testing. I can fund updates myself through December 2026. Updates from January 2027 onward, 8.0 support, and further work on Full Remake will depend on community support.

If UXBA is useful to you, a contribution at a level you are comfortable with would help keep the project going. Messages of encouragement are welcome too.

- [Support on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lost2)
- [Support on OFUSE](https://ofuse.me/lost)

Please use the repository's Issues and Discussions for bug reports and feature conversations.
