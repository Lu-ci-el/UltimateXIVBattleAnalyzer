# Release information

## 3.8.2.7 (changes since 3.7.9.8)

- **Enemy Timeline** shows casts, hit timing, targets and boss HP in order. Choose a start and end to save a replay DAT of that scene or export the event list as CSV or JSON.
- **Appearance Anonymization** lets you choose weapon and armor series independently for the party. Search for `Ultimate` to find seven reward series, including Ultima. Search by relic family (ZW, AW, EW, RW, MW or FW) to choose an upgrade stage. You can also select job-specific armor or randomize each player’s race, face and hair color.
- **Full Remake** lets you edit individual party members’ appearances and equipment and save the changes in a replay DAT.
- **Party role labels** can be swapped for T1/T2, H1/H2 and D1/D2. The recorder’s filename uses the chosen label. Tank job choices keep the two players in their original job-priority order.

See the [English user guide](USER_GUIDE.en.md) for instructions and the [developer’s note](../README.en.md#future-updates) for plans for 7.58 and beyond.

## 3.7.9.8

- Added action removal and job selection within the same role, with cleaner friendly effects, buffs, and shield displays.
- Set anonymized damage and healing displays to zero and adjusted death, revival, and recorder presentation.
- Shortened the wait before extracted battles to at most 5 seconds and added a 0% setting for "Cut ending."
- Added progress and cancellation controls while saving.
- Shortened output names to formats such as `Re-D4 NA La(M) S PH Hd_on Sp10 Anon`.
- Added the optional "Contingency update" feature.

See the [English user guide](USER_GUIDE.en.md) for instructions.

## Initial public release

Includes replay DAT inspection, ACT log extraction, anonymization and appearance changes, recorder selection, tail cutting, battle extraction, character-ID replacement, supported older-DAT conversion, and individual ZIP compression.

See the [English user guide](USER_GUIDE.en.md) for instructions and supported conditions.
